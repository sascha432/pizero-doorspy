Camera is Frigate's enable switch for this camera: off stops its ffmpeg decoding and the stream pull from the Zero, so Camera = PIR is what keeps the Pi 4 idle while nobody is at the door (Frigate needs a few seconds to start the stream after a trigger). The other three only matter while the camera is on; leave them at Always unless you want finer control. Person detection needs motion detection. The badge shows what Frigate reports.
